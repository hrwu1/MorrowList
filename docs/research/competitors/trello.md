# Trello (Atlassian) — competitor report for MorrowList

Researched 2026-09-02. Every row cites a source; items I could not verify are marked _unverified_.

## 1. Product snapshot

- **Who uses it:** the archetypal Kanban board, used by individuals and small teams (marketing, digital marketing, software dev are the top verticals per 6sense). Since the May 22, 2025 redesign Atlassian explicitly repositions Trello as a **personal productivity / to-do tool** ("capture → organize → tackle"), steering heavy PM use toward Jira ([Atlassian blog](https://www.atlassian.com/blog/announcements/new-trello-is-here), [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2025/08/06/trello_redesign_as_bad_as/)). This is the same persona MorrowList targets.
- **Pricing (Sept 2026):** Free ($0; 10 collaborators, 10 boards/Workspace, unlimited cards, 10 MB/file, 250 automation runs/mo, unlimited Power-Ups, Inbox); Standard $5/user/mo annual ($6 monthly; unlimited boards, Planner full calendar, AI capture, mirror cards, Custom Fields, advanced checklists, 250 MB/file, 1,000 runs, list colors/collapse); Premium $10 ($12.50 monthly; Calendar/Timeline/Table/Dashboard/Map views, Workspace views, unlimited runs, templates, CSV export); Enterprise $17.50 annual ([trello.com/pricing](https://trello.com/pricing)).
- **Scale:** ~90M registered users, ~1M MAU (secondary estimates; Atlassian does not disclose Trello-only metrics) ([sqmagazine](https://sqmagazine.co.uk/trello-statistics/)); 30M+ Android downloads; ~10.4M monthly web visits (Semrush, Feb 2026).
- **Ratings:** G2 4.4/5 (13,000+ reviews) ([findstack](https://findstack.com/products/trello/reviews), [taskrhino](https://www.taskrhino.ca/blog/trello-review/)); Capterra 4.5/5 (23,599 reviews; ease of use 4.5, support 4.3) ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/211559/Trello/reviews/)); Trustpilot 2.2/5 (224 reviews, mostly billing/cancellation complaints) ([Trustpilot](https://jp.trustpilot.com/review/trello.com)).

## 3. Gaps MorrowList should consider

**Table-stakes** (expected from any Kanban tool; Capterra reviewers list "checklists, due dates, attachments, and labels" as the baseline value of a card):

1. **Labels/tags** (row 5) — Trello's primary categorization; Capterra pros cite them; ML has Tags planned. Include colorblind mode.
2. **Checklists / subtasks UI** (6) — 2026 AI push is built on them (AI-generated checklists); parent_id exists in ML schema.
3. **Column editing** (1) — add/rename/reorder/color/collapse; Trello made list colors and collapse a paid feature, so ML shipping them free is a small differentiator.
4. **Filter UI + global search with operators** (26, 27) — Trello's `due:overdue`, `@me`, `-has:` operators are heavily used; ML has the URL params, needs the UI and free-text search.
5. **Archive + undo + bulk** (36, 37, 39) — Trello users rely on `c`/`z`; ML has undo and bulk planned. Note Trello caps multi-select at 20 cards.
6. **Due-date reminders + notifications** (4, 40) — Trello requires explicitly setting a reminder and being a member/watcher, a common confusion; ML could default to a sensible reminder.
7. **Start date** (4) — needed for Calendar/Timeline later.
8. **Keyboard shortcut vocabulary** (35) — `j/k/n/t/c/d/l/f/x/z`; cheap and prominent in Trello's "pro" docs.
9. **JSON export AND import** (52, 53) — Trello cannot import its own export; a Trello-JSON importer is a concrete migration wedge for a self-hosted tool.
10. **Activity history UI** (43) — ML already persists events.

**Differentiators** (help ML stand out for the personal-first persona): 11. **Capture channels into Inbox** (17, 44, 56) — email-to-inbox, browser extension, share sheet. Trello's own community said "Inbox is cool (about the only thing they changed that was good)" ([Substack](https://trello.substack.com/p/is-it-time-to-leave-trello)). Email-in is free on Trello. 12. **Calendar/Today planning** (19, 20) — Planner (time-blocking against Google/Outlook) is Trello's flagship 2025 bet; ML's Today/My Day + a calendar feed (ICS) or sync would cover the personal use case; Trello puts board Calendar view behind Premium. 13. **Automation rules** (28–31) — Butler is praised ("powerful for simple workflows, no code") but quota-capped (250/1,000 runs/mo) and users hit limits "in week 2" ([taskrhino](https://www.taskrhino.ca/blog/trello-review/)). Unlimited self-hosted rules (incl. HTTP request action) is a clear win. 14. **Recurring tasks natively** (32) — Trello needs a Power-Up plus an automation to shift dates; reviewers call recurring tasks a gap. 15. **Webhooks + MCP server** (48, 49) — Trello shipped an official MCP in July 2026 free for all; ML's API/CLI-first design plus MCP would target the same "agent" persona with local data. 16. **Dependencies / blocked-by** (16) — repeatedly cited as Trello's "glaring gap" (taskrhino, Capterra, thefrontkit); ML has it planned. 17. **Table view with inline edit + saved views** (22, 25) — Premium-only in Trello. 18. **Custom fields** (10) — Standard+; ML's priority field already covers the most common one.

**Nice-to-have:** card covers/attachments (8–9), templates (34), card aging (33), dashboard charts (23), mirror cards (15), AI writing/checklist/due-date-from-title (50–51; the last is doable with a date parser), desktop app with global quick-add hotkey (55).

**Niche:** Map view (24), board visibility/public boards (57), Slack/Teams/Jira integrations (45).

## 4. What users complain about (opportunities)

- **The 2025 redesign.** "Arguably one of the worst in the past five years, and possibly in tech history"; ~200-post community thread; Reddit "Open letter to Trello leadership"; card back has "FOUR different places to look for what used to be one column of buttons"; floating bottom bar hides the scrollbar; adding members takes more clicks ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2025/08/06/trello_redesign_as_bad_as/), [Substack](https://trello.substack.com/p/is-it-time-to-leave-trello)). Opportunity: a stable, dense, one-column card detail; don't move things.
- **Direction: "AI-powered personal to-do list, not a project management tool."** Team users feel pushed to Jira ([Substack](https://trello.substack.com/p/is-it-time-to-leave-trello)). ML's personal-first-then-teams path can serve both if the board model stays intact.
- **Free tier walls:** 10 boards / 10 collaborators (boards go view-only above 10 collaborators since May 2024), 250 automation runs, views behind Premium ([everhour](https://everhour.com/blog/trello-free-vs-paid/), [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/211559/Trello/reviews/)). Reddit: "free limitations are too much and the upgrades are ridiculously expensive."
- **No dependencies, no reporting, no sprints, weak cross-board visibility**; "power-up duct tape only stretches so far" ([thefrontkit](https://thefrontkit.com/blogs/trello-alternatives-2026), [taskrhino](https://www.taskrhino.ca/blog/trello-review/)).
- **Clutter at scale:** boards become "chaotic"; Power-Up reliance adds cost and UI clutter ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/211559/Trello/reviews/)).
- **No import, lossy export:** JSON keeps only 1000 actions; CSV drops comments; cannot re-import ([export doc](https://support.atlassian.com/trello/docs/exporting-data-from-trello/)).
- **Deletion is permanent; undo only own actions; concurrent description edits can be erased** ([undo doc](https://support.atlassian.com/trello/docs/undoing-an-action-in-trello/)) — ML's Trash + optimistic versioning already beats this.
- **Billing/cancellation, account deletion, AI-training data concerns, forced logouts** dominate Trustpilot (2.2/5) ([Trustpilot](https://jp.trustpilot.com/review/trello.com)). Self-hosting sidesteps all of these.
- **Accessibility:** drag-and-drop "a real barrier" for keyboard/screen-reader users; VPAT dated 2019/2022 and PDF-only ([community](https://community.atlassian.com/forums/Trello-questions/How-accessible-is-Trello/qaq-p/1523750)). ML's keyboard drag + Move menu is a genuine edge worth marketing.
