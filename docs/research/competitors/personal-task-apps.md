# Personal task managers with a board view — competitor research for MorrowList

Date: 2026-09-02. Method: primary sources (help centers, pricing/feature pages, changelogs, developer docs) plus independent reviews (Capterra, Trustpilot, AlternativeTo, Asian Efficiency, HabitBox, Morgen, alfred\_, The Business Dive, Saner.ai). Reddit was not fetchable from this environment; review-site "cons" sections are used as the user-voice proxy. Claims I could not confirm are marked **unverified**. Web-search budget ran out mid-task, so Zenkit To Do, MeisterTask paid tiers and Nozbe board view are thinner than the rest.

## 1. Product snapshots

**Todoist (Doist)** — Mass-market personal task manager, "30+ million users" (todoist.com/pricing). Beginner (free: 5 projects, 3 filters, 1-week history), Pro ($5/mo annual, $7 monthly after a Dec-2025 rise from $4), Business $8/user. Web, macOS, Windows, Linux, iOS, Android, watch, browser extensions, email add-ons. Capterra 4.6/5 from 2,662 reviews. Board, list and calendar layouts per project/filter; official REST+Sync API, webhooks, MCP server, open-source CLI.

**TickTick (Appest)** — All-in-one "tasks + calendar + habits + Pomodoro". Free (9 lists, 99 tasks/list, 2 reminders/task, list + Kanban views) vs Premium $49.99/yr on the upgrade page (reviews quote $35.99/yr — region-dependent). iOS, Android, Mac, Windows, Linux, web, watch, browser extensions. Capterra 4.7/5 (129 reviews). Open API exists but reviewers call it "very basic".

**Microsoft To Do + Planner** — Bundled with Microsoft 365; To Do (free, consumer) owns My Day/suggestions/steps; the "new Planner" (2025–26) puts individuals' My Day and My Tasks in a Board view by default and adds Copilot, task chat and custom templates; premium Plan 1/3/5 licences for goals/sprints/portfolios. iCalendar feed to external calendars was retired in early 2026.

**Things 3 (Cultured Code)** — Apple-only GTD benchmark; one-time purchase $49.99 Mac + $9.99 iPhone/Watch + $19.99 iPad (~$80 total, no subscription). Today/This Evening, Upcoming, Anytime, Someday, Areas, headings, checklists, tags, deadlines vs "when" dates, Quick Entry hotkey, Mail to Things, URL scheme; no web, Windows, Android, API or collaboration.

**OmniFocus 4 (Omni Group)** — Power-user GTD: perspectives, Forecast, Review, defer + due dates, tags, flags, real-world repeat rules, Omni Automation. $74.99 Standard / $149.99 Pro one-time, or $99.99/yr incl. web. Apple platforms + web. App Store 4.1/5 (327 ratings); praised depth, criticised price/learning curve.

**Sunsama** — Guided daily-planning app that pulls tasks from Todoist/Asana/Jira/Notion/Gmail/Slack etc. into a day view with time-boxing, focus mode, shutdown ritual, weekly review, rollover, backlog and a Kanban board. $17–22/mo (pricing page) with reviews quoting $20–25 after a 2026 rise; no free plan; Web, macOS, Windows, Linux, iOS, Android; MCP + Zapier.

**Akiflow** — Command-bar-driven time-blocking planner with universal inbox from 50+ tools, rituals, availability sharing, "Aki" AI + MCP. $34/mo or $19/mo annual, no free tier, 7-day trial.

**Amazing Marvin** — Solo "build-your-own-system" planner with 100+ toggleable strategies (day planner, time blocking, Procrastination Wizard, Pomodoro, habits, rewards). $12/mo or $8/mo annual, Web/Mac/Windows/iOS/Android. Public API + webhooks + direct CouchDB sync credentials.

**Superlist** — Wunderlist successor (tasks + notes), infinitely nested lists, AI meeting notes, voice, offline, real-time sharing. Free (5 lists), Basic $5/person/mo ($59/yr), Super $21/mo ($249/yr). macOS, iOS, Android, Web. App Store 4.5/5 (379 ratings). No board view found (**unverified**).

**Zenkit To Do / Zenkit Projects** — To Do is a Wunderlist-style list app (smart lists Starred/Today/This Week, reminders, repeats, sharing); Kanban/Gantt/mind-map only in Zenkit Projects (Plus €8, Team €12, Business €21 per seat). To Do pricing page returned 404 — **unverified**.

**MeisterTask** — Kanban-first team tool; Free: 3 projects, unlimited tasks, 20 AI credits; recurring tasks, automations, custom fields and API are paid. Paid prices not visible in fetched page — **unverified**.

**Nozbe** — GTD-derived team/personal app; Free: 3 projects, 3 sections/tags, 7-day history, no private projects; Premium from $6/member/mo. Web, iOS, Android, Windows, macOS. Board/kanban rendering of sections **unverified**.

**Trello (personal use)** — 2025 "new Trello" added an Inbox column on every board, capture via Siri/Slack/email (inbox@app.trello.com), AI extraction of dates/priorities, card mirroring, and a Planner calendar view. Free: 10 boards, 10 collaborators, 250 automation runs/mo; Standard $5 unlocks Planner + unlimited boards; Premium $10 adds Calendar/Timeline/Table/Dashboard views.

## 4. What users complain about — openings for MorrowList

- **Subscription fatigue and price creep.** Todoist Pro rose 25% in Dec 2025; Sunsama raised prices in 2026 ("the most expensive subscription I have"); Akiflow $34/mo with no free tier; Marvin has no free plan. Things' one-time $80 is repeatedly praised as "cheaper than Todoist by year two". A self-hosted, one-price/free tool speaks directly to this.
- **Free-tier walls on core features.** Todoist free: 5 projects, no reminders, 3 filters, 1-week history; TickTick free: 9 lists, 99 tasks/list, 2 reminders; Nozbe/MeisterTask: 3 projects. Capterra Todoist reviewers: "useful features are only available in the paid version."
- **Data lock-in / portability.** Things: "only syncs with its own sync service … a walled garden inside walled garden", "no native export"; TickTick "cloud-dependent"; Planner retired its iCal feed in 2026. Full JSON export + API + Postgres ownership is a clean counter-position.
- **Weak or missing API.** Things has only a URL scheme; TickTick's API is "only very basic"; MeisterTask/Zenkit gate API behind Business tiers. ML's API/CLI parity is already ahead.
- **Board view is second-class in list-first apps.** Todoist board: subtasks invisible, not available in Upcoming on mobile, no swimlanes; Zenkit To Do and Superlist have no board at all; Trello has boards but no real task model (dates/priorities only via AI or Power-Ups). A board with WIP limits, statuses with categories and keyboard drag is a differentiator.
- **Platform lock.** Things/OmniFocus are Apple-only (TechRadar, AlternativeTo cons); Superlist/Sunsama lack some native surfaces. A good PWA covers Windows/Linux/Android users ignored by the GTD gold-standard apps.
- **Notifications and sync reliability.** Todoist and TickTick both draw complaints about missed reminders and occasional sync delays; TickTick draws Trustpilot billing/cancellation complaints and slow bug fixes.
- **Feature bloat vs simplicity.** TickTick: "desktop app can feel overwhelming"; Todoist: "too complex and structured" for simple needs; Marvin: first week "overwhelming". Keeping one task model with a few views is a selling point if the daily loop is strong.
- **Design-imposed limits.** Todoist refuses start dates; Sunsama caps planning at two weeks; Planner "flat workflows", limited automation; To Do/Planner split confuses individuals.
