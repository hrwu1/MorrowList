# Competitor research: Asana and monday.com (plus Basecamp, Wrike) vs MorrowList

Date: 2026-09-02. Baseline: `BASELINE.md`. "MorrowList has it?" uses Yes / Partial / No / Planned (spec'd, not built). Claims I could not verify from a fetched source are marked _unverified_.

## 1. Product snapshots

**Asana** — Work-management platform for mid-size and enterprise teams (marketing, ops, PMOs); "170,000+ customers" ([G2 seller page via search][s-g2-asana]). G2 4.4/5 on ~14,000 reviews ([G2][s-g2-asana]). Pricing: Personal free (2 users, unlimited tasks/projects, no custom fields/rules/timeline); Starter $10.99/user/mo annual ($13.49 monthly); Advanced $24.99 ($30.49); Enterprise/Enterprise+ custom ([asana.com/pricing][asana-pricing]). 2026 releases centre on AI Studio / AI Teammates, a flowchart rule builder, and a Timesheets & Budgets add-on ([Winter 2026][asana-winter], [Spring 2026][asana-spring]). Reviewers note it has the fewest performance complaints of the big three but a steep Starter→Advanced "pricing cliff" ([Rock][rock-asana], [Efficient App search snippet][s-asana-reviews]).

**monday.com** — Highly visual, spreadsheet-like "Work OS" (boards → groups → items → subitems, 30+ column types) for SMB through enterprise; ~250,000 customers, 65,783 paid customers with >10 users, Q2 2026 revenue $364.6M (+22% YoY), NDR 109% ([Q2 2026 results][mnd-q2], [Rock][rock-monday]). G2 4.7/5 on ~15–19k reviews ([G2 learn][g2-monday], [G2 seller via search][s-g2-monday]). Pricing: Free (2 seats, 3 boards); Basic $9/seat annual; Standard $12; Pro $19; Enterprise custom; automations 250/mo on Standard, 25k on Pro; Timeline/Gantt Standard+; time tracking, dependencies, private boards Pro+ ([monday.com/pricing][mnd-pricing]). Widely reported 3-seat minimum on paid plans ([Rock][rock-monday], [Cloudwards][cloudwards]). 2026 AI: Sidekick, Agents, Vibe (prompt-to-app), MCP server, AI Blocks; credit-based since July 2026 ([monday AI blog][mnd-ai]).

**Basecamp** (brief) — Flat-priced team tool from 37signals. Card Table = its Kanban: Triage row + "Not Now" column + custom colour-coded columns with optional "On Hold" sub-area; cards have assignees, due dates, steps (subtasks), comments; watch a column for new cards; "wormholes" move cards to other tables ([Basecamp help][bc-help], [basecamp.com/features][bc-features]). Pricing: Free (1 project, 5 users), Freelancer $25/mo, Studio $59, Pro $99, Unlimited $299/mo billed annually — no per-user fees ([basecamp.com/pricing][bc-pricing]).

**Wrike** (brief) — Enterprise PMO/creative-ops tool. Board view on all plans; columns from workflow status _or_ single/multi-select custom field; swimlanes by assignee or custom field; configurable card fields incl. cover images; no WIP limits documented ([Wrike help][wrike-board]). Pricing: Free; Team $10/user (2–15 users); Business $25/user (5–200); Pinnacle/Apex custom ([wrike.com/price][wrike-price]). Reviewers call the UI dated and the Spaces > Folders > Projects hierarchy heavy ([search summary][s-wrike]).

## 3. Gaps MorrowList should consider

Ratings: **T** = table-stakes, **D** = differentiator, **N** = nice-to-have, **X** = niche (for a personal-first Kanban).

1. **Subtasks UI** (Planned) — Both products treat decomposition as core; monday's inability to show subitems on Kanban drew a 5-year, 91-reply thread ([mnd-subitem-kanban]). Do it _and_ show subtasks on the board. **T**
2. **Today / My Tasks with date-driven auto-move** (Planned) — Asana's My Tasks rules (move to Today when due today) and monday's My Work buckets are the daily driver for individuals ([s-asana-mytasks], [simpleday]). **T**
3. **Search + saved views** (Planned / long-term) — monday users have asked for years for personal saved views ([mnd-savedview]); Asana only shipped per-user saved views in late 2024 ([asana-savedview]). Personal-first means every saved view is personal by default. **T / D**
4. **Recurring tasks** (long-term) — Native in Asana with "periodically after completion"; monday needs an automation or paid app ([s-asana-recur], [mnd-recur]). For personal use this is table-stakes. **T**
5. **Custom fields / tags** (Tags planned) — Asana gates custom fields to Starter; monday's whole model is columns. At least tags + a small set of typed fields. **T**
6. **Calendar view** (long-term) — Free on both. **T**
7. **Swimlanes / group-by on board** — Asana gated it to Starter+ and shipped only in 2025; monday has "Divide by" ([asana-swimlanes], [mnd-kanban]). Cheap to add given workflow statuses + priority/project already exist. **D**
8. **Card display customisation and cover images** — Both allow choosing card fields; Asana has cover images ([usecarly-board], [asana-cover]). **N**
9. **Simple rules** — Both gate automations to paid tiers and cap counts (Asana 20 rules/project on Starter; monday 250 actions/mo on Standard), a top complaint ([asana-rules-limit], [mnd-pricing]). Even 5 personal rules (due-date → Today, status Done → complete) with no caps would be a differentiator. **D**
10. **Dependencies / blocked-by** (Planned) — Gated to Starter (Asana) and Pro (monday). **N** for personal, **T** for teams.
11. **CSV/JSON import & export** (export planned) — Both offer CSV in and out; import matters for switchers from Asana/monday/Trello. **T**
12. **Activity history UI + undo** (Partial) — Both show activity feeds; MorrowList already stores events. **N**
13. **Webhooks + MCP/agent access** (long-term) — Both now expose an MCP surface ([asana-winter], [mnd-ai]); MorrowList's CLI/API is a natural fit. **D**
14. **Offline / PWA** (Planned) — Both offer partial offline with naive conflict handling; MorrowList's versioning could do better. **D**
15. **Keyboard shortcuts** (Partial) — Asana's Tab-chord system is loved by power users ([asana-keys]). **N**
16. **Templates** — **N**. **Time tracking** — **X** (gated to $19–25/user in both). **Dashboards, workload, portfolios, goals, forms, proofing, approvals** — **X** for the MVP persona.

## 4. What users complain about (opportunities)

- **Pricing cliffs and seat minimums.** monday's 3-seat minimum makes a solo user pay $27/mo on Basic, and "most teams need Pro" ($57/mo for three seats) ([rock-monday], [cloudwards]). Asana's Starter→Advanced jump more than doubles per-user cost and gates time tracking, goals, portfolios ([rock-asana]). → MorrowList: self-host, no seats.
- **Feature gating and usage caps.** Custom fields, rules, dependencies and timeline are paid on Asana; automations are absent on monday Basic and capped at 250/mo on Standard ([asana-pricing], [mnd-pricing], [zapier]). Asana's rule-per-project limits frustrate even premium customers ([asana-rules-limit]). → Ship small, uncapped rules.
- **Bloat / overwhelm.** monday's "density of statuses, colors, and columns" and "you're responsible for building the entire system"; Asana "feels cluttered as projects grow" ([rock-monday], [s-asana-reviews], [g2-monday]). → Keep one canonical task model and three views.
- **Mobile lags desktop** on both ([rock-asana], [rock-monday], [cloudwards]). → A good responsive/PWA experience is a differentiator.
- **Performance at scale**: monday boards slow with many items ([cloudwards]); Asana is the exception ([s-asana-reviews]).
- **Rigid model choices**: Asana's one assignee per task (open since 2017, 173 likes) ([asana-assignee]); monday subitems not on Kanban ([mnd-subitem-kanban]); board-wide saved views ([mnd-savedview]).
- **Naive offline conflicts**: monday "yours will override theirs" ([mnd-offline]). MorrowList's optimistic versioning already handles this better.
- **Fragmented AI pricing**: monday's AI has "separate pricing" and credit metering since July 2026; Asana's AI Studio is credit-limited with paid add-ons ([zapier], [mnd-ai], [s-asana-ai]). → Bring-your-own-model via CLI/API rather than metered AI.
- **Support**: monday "hard to access a human agent" ([cloudwards]).
